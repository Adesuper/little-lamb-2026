📖 How to use this app (1 minute, read first)
  • Type in the mascot's name. Tap Full Screen, then Let's Start!. There are 11 pages; go forward with Next ➡ or the → key.
  • Page 4 (the song) plays what-a-miracle.mp3, which must sit in the same folder as this app. Without it, just sing together.
  • Page 6 (Whose Voice?) uses the laptop microphone: click Allow when the browser asks. Recordings stay on this screen only and are gone when you close it.
  • Real games with right/wrong answers: I Am Special, Whose Voice?, Fingerprint Twins, Fruit or Vegetable? and Who Helps?. Let a different child take each turn.
  • The app supports the Leader's Guide (pp. 49–54). The paper-bag puppets, discovery stations, dress-up box and crayons are still the lesson.
