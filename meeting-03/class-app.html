📖 How to use this app (1 minute, read first)
  • Type in the mascot's name — the app uses it on the pages.
  • Tap Full Screen, then Let's Start! — 11 pages. Next with the green Next ➡ button or the → key.
  • Real games with right/wrong answers: Rock Thumbs, Rock Hunt, Big or Little? Heavy or Light?, Sink or Float?, Colour Sort, Build on the Rock! and Story in Order. Let a different child take each turn.
  • The app supports the Leader's Guide (pp. 43–48); the real rocks, sand tubs, magnifying glasses, water bowls and glue are still the lesson. The pages follow the lesson plan's order.
