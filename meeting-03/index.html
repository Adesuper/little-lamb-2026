Before you go

  1. Test that your lava rock floats (a pharmacy pumice stone always does) and try the rain test at home.
  2. Collect rocks of all kinds, play sand, sieves, magnifiers, a kitchen scale, cardboard squares and tacky glue.
  3. Arrive 25 minutes early: hide the Rock Hunt rocks, bury treasure in the sand tubs, set out the five centres.
