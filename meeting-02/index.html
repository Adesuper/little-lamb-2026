Before you go

  1. Draw the white-crayon lambs, pressing hard, and test-paint one the night before.
  2. Collect large t-shirts, pillowcases, ribbon, toy sheep, sticks and a baby bottle.
  3. Arrive 20 minutes early: set up the tents, tape the line, hide the lamb puppet.
